Design previews

Three directions for the National Health League app, each shown as Home and Lab Results. Same content throughout — only the design changes, so they compare fairly. Nothing here is connected to live data.

Option 1
Clinical Calm

Warm white, one teal accent, generous space. A circular score dial, with the two markers needing attention lifted above the rest. Quiet and reassuring.

Home → Lab Results →
Option 2
Paper & Ink

Warm paper with a dark score band and a violet accent. Serif headlines, a split focus-and-ledger layout, and a bento grid for resources. Editorial in feel.

Home → Lab Results →
Option 3
Vital

Deep forest green with a lime accent, on cool sage paper. A large score gauge, every marker as its own tile with a progress bar. Closest to a modern consumer health app.

These are visual mockups only — buttons and forms are not wired up, and the numbers shown are sample values. The live app is unchanged.
