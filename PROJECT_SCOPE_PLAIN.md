Project summary — plain language (8th-grade)

What is this?
- A simple app that helps employees learn about health tests and find trusted videos and articles.

Who is it for?
- Employees who want clear, reliable health info.
- Employers who want a safe wellness tool for staff.

What you do in the app
- Type in a lab test name and a number (you choose to do this).
- The app shows short videos, articles, and expert links that match your input.
- It gives education, not medical advice.

Important rules (privacy)
- The app does NOT save your personal health numbers.
- All lab numbers you enter stay only on your device.
- The app only shows educational content, never diagnoses or treatment plans.

How the app is kept safe
- Each employer sees only their own branded content.
- The team reviews all health content before it’s shown.

Short elevator pitch
- "An easy, private place for employees to learn about lab tests — not medical advice."
