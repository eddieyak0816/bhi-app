Navigation Bar

The navigation bar sits at the top of every page in the app. It remains fixed as you scroll so you can always switch pages quickly.

Navigation bar — dark mode

ElementWhat it does
BHI logoAlways visible on the left. Does not navigate anywhere — it is a brand identifier only.
HomeReturns you to the Dashboard from any page. The active page is highlighted in blue with an underline.
LibraryOpens the Resource Library where you can browse and search all health resources.
CategoriesBrowse resources grouped by health category (e.g., Cardiovascular, Nutrition, Fitness).
Lab ResultsOpens the Labs page for entering, uploading, or reviewing your lab values.
ProfileOpens your Profile page where you manage personal details, biometrics, and privacy settings.
AdminOnly visible to users with admin or super-admin roles. Opens the Admin CMS panel.
UsernameYour display name shown in the top-right corner, confirming which account is active.
☀️ / 🌙 buttonToggles between dark mode and light mode. Your preference is saved automatically and persists between sessions.
LogoutSigns you out of the app immediately and returns you to the login screen.

Stale Lab Data Warning

If your most recent lab results are more than 180 days old, an amber warning banner appears directly below the welcome header.

Stale lab banner — shown when results are over 6 months old

Lab results are 195 days old
For accurate health scoring, update your labs every 6 months.
  • The banner shows exactly how many days have passed since your last lab entry.
  • Clicking Log New Results takes you directly to the Labs page.
  • The banner disappears automatically once you save new lab results.
Why 180 days? BHI recommends updating lab values at least every 6 months so your BHAS score and personalized recommendations remain accurate and clinically meaningful.

BHAS Score Banner

Once you have logged at least one lab result, the BHAS (Balanced Health Assessment Score) banner appears. This is your primary health score, calculated using standard clinical thresholds for each marker you have entered.

BHAS Score banner — showing a 78% score with per-marker chips

Left side — Overall percentage:

ColorScore rangeMeaning
Green80% and aboveMost entered markers are in the optimal clinical range.
Amber50% – 79%Good progress, but several markers have room for improvement.
RedBelow 50%Multiple markers are outside the optimal range and may need attention.

Right side — Per-marker chips:

Chip colorSymbolMeaningPoints
Green✓Optimal — value is within the ideal clinical range.1.0
Amber~Improvement — outside optimal but not critically so.0.5
Red✗Out of range — falls outside the acceptable threshold.0
Tip Hover over any chip to see the specific health tag that fired for that marker (e.g., Normal_Glucose). This tells you exactly why a marker received its score.
Note Missing markers are not penalized — the score is a percentage of what you have entered. Entering more markers gives a more complete and useful picture.

BHAS v2.3 Advanced Score Panel

When you have entered enough data for at least 4 of the 9 scoreable metrics, a second panel appears below the standard BHAS banner. This is the BHAS v2.3 score — a clinically advanced model that calculates derived ratios from your raw values and biometric data.

Score labels:

LabelScore rangeMeaning
Optimal8.0 – 9.0Excellent — most metrics are in their ideal range.

Needs Improvement4.0 – 5.9Several metrics are below optimal.
High RiskBelow 4.0Multiple metrics need immediate attention.

The 9 v2.3 metrics:

MetricDerived fromOptimal threshold
HOMA-IRFasting Glucose × Fasting Insulin ÷ 405< 1.5 = 1 pt; 1.5–2.5 = 0.5 pt
TG/HDL RatioTriglycerides ÷ HDL Cholesterol< 1.5 = 1 pt; 1.5–3.0 = 0.5 pt
Grip RatioGrip strength (kg) ÷ Body weight (kg)≥ 0.45 male / ≥ 0.35 female = 1 pt
Waist-to-Height (WtHR)Waist circumference ÷ Height (cm)< 0.50 = 1 pt; 0.50–0.60 = 0.5 pt
Vitamin DDirect lab value> 50 ng/mL = 1 pt
Vitamin B12Direct lab value> 750 pg/mL = 1 pt
VO2 Max PercentileEntered directly (0–100)≥ 60th percentile = 1 pt
hs-CRPDirect lab value< 1.0 mg/L = 1 pt; 1.0–3.0 = 0.5 pt
Advanced Care PlanCheckbox on ProfileDocumented = 1 pt
Type 1 Diabetes substitution If you have checked Type 1 Diabetes on your Profile, HOMA-IR is replaced by Insulin Units per kg (Total Daily Insulin ÷ Body Weight). The total is still out of 9.0.
Tip Hover over any metric chip to see the derived value used (e.g., HOMA-IR: 1.4). You never need to calculate these manually — just enter your raw lab values and the app does it automatically.

If some metrics are missing, a line at the bottom of the panel lists exactly what data is needed, with direct links to Profile or Labs to fill it in.

Each card shows:

  • 📌 Recommended — confirms this was matched specifically to your profile.
  • Resource type — Article, Video, PDF Guide, etc.
  • Title — the name of the resource.
  • Health tags — the tags that linked this resource to you.
  • View Resource — opens the full resource.
How matching works The scoring engine evaluates your lab values against clinical thresholds and assigns health tags (e.g., Low_VitD). Resources that have been tagged with those same labels are then surfaced here. More markers entered = more specific recommendations.
