Comparison

  • Design 1 (Horizontal): All filters visible and pills below. Takes ~80-100px height. Good balance.
  • Design 2 (Accordion): Filters collapsed by default (~40px), expands to show all options. Pills always visible. Most compact when closed.
  • Design 3 (Trigger Button): Minimal footprint (~36px default with pills). Opens modal/sidebar for selecting multiple options. Best space savings.
