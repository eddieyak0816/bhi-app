Onboarding screens
1) What this app does
   - Short text: Type a lab test and a number. We show short videos and easy articles that explain the result.

   - Short text: Your numbers stay on your device. We do not save them.
3) How to use
   - Short text: Enter a test name (example: Vitamin D), type the number, then tap "See resources."

Help / FAQ (short Q&A)
- Q: Is this medical advice?
  A: No. This is learning material only.
- Q: Do you save my numbers?
  A: No. Your numbers stay on your device unless you choose to save them.
- Q: Who checks the videos?
  A: Experts review everything before it’s shown.
